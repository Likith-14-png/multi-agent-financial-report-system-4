Management Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
Vantage Retail Corp operates a chain of specialty home-goods stores across the
mid-Atlantic region. FY2024 same-store sales grew modestly as the company
completed a store-refresh program. Management's outlook for FY2025 calls for
continued steady growth, supported by an expanded e-commerce channel and
disciplined cost control, with no major store openings planned.
