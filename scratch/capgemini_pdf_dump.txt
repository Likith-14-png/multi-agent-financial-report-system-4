Key Company Information: 
●​ Global Presence: Over 420,000 experts working across more than 50 countries. 
●​ Workforce Demographics: More than 160 different nationalities; over 40% 
women. 
●​ Business Model: A global leader in business and technology transformation, 
powered by AI and driven by close to 60 years of innovation. 
●​ Strategic Focus: Embedding AI into everything, delivering sovereign cloud 
leadership, and expanding capabilities in data and AI. 
●​ Acquisitions: Acquired WNS (a leader in digital Business Process Services) for 
$3.3 billion, and Cloud4C (a provider of hybrid, private, public, and sovereign cloud 
services). 
●​ Innovation: Network of 21 Applied Innovation Exchanges (AIE). 
●​ Training: 97 average hours of training per employee in 2025; 310,000 employees 
trained in Generative AI and 194,000 in Agentic AI (excluding WNS). 
●​ Sustainability Goal: Net zero business by 2040; 100% renewable electricity used 
for all operations in 2025.

Identified Risks and Operational Challenges 
●​ Macroeconomic Environment: The report notes a "volatile economic 
environment" with "client demand proved quite selective in 2025, with a strong 

----- PAGE 11 -----
focus on efficiency, operational agility, and cost optimization." There is specific 
mention of a "challenging market, particularly in Europe." 
●​ Cybersecurity & Data Protection Risk: Emphasized as a critical area. The Group 
mandates training to mitigate risk; as of December 31, 2025, 96% of the workforce 
had completed a mandatory Cybersecurity training module, and 94% completed the 
data protection module. 98% of operation centers and sensitive facilities are ISO 
27001 certified. 
●​ Talent Retention & Skills Gap: The rapid evolution of AI necessitates massive 
reskilling to avoid operational obsolescence. Capgemini mitigated this by training 
310,000 employees in Generative AI and 194,000 in Agentic AI. 
●​ Technological Sovereignty: The report highlights geopolitical pressures 
accelerating the need for "diversification and resilience strategies" and "sovereign 
alternatives" in cloud and AI deployments.
