Management Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
Orion Steelworks Ltd is a mid-cap industrial steel producer. Fiscal year 2024 was
marked by softer construction demand and elevated input costs. Management expects
volumes to stabilize in FY2025 as infrastructure spending resumes, though pricing
pressure from imports remains a risk. The outlook for FY2025 is cautiously positive,
contingent on raw material cost trends and demand recovery in the construction sector.
